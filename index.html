Classical Attacks on Peaked Quantum Circuits

2570 / 2570 Solved Preprint in Preparation Quantum Advantage Tracker

Peaked random circuit sampling (Aaronson–Zhang) and heuristic peaked circuit benchmarks (HQAP, arXiv:2510.25838) were designed as quantum supremacy challenges where random-looking circuits concentrate output probability onto a secret target bitstring. We developed structural de-obfuscation and compressed tensor-network methods that recover the secret peak classically without reading target bitstrings at runtime.

Using these tools, we solved all 12 problem instances on the BlueQubit Peak Portal challenge (2,570/2,570 points, first-time correct). On instance P9 (56 qubits), our middle-out operator solver finds the peak in approximately 3 seconds on a laptop, compared to approximately 1 hour on an NVIDIA A100 GPU reported by prior published methods (Kremer & Dupuis, arXiv:2604.21908). On P6 (62 qubits), we solved the instance by discovering ring-partitioned star tensor-network contractions where gap layers factor into ring-local clusters.

qsim-lab: Exact Quantum Circuit Simulator

Rust Python (qsimlab) Differential Tested

An exact quantum circuit simulation engine written from scratch in Rust, with Python bindings (qsimlab via PyO3). Every engine is strictly exact (no unstated truncation or approximation) and differential-tested against independent reference state vectors. Built from scratch in Rust, with components developed alongside AI coding agents and verified through rigorous differential testing and audit logs.

Gate-Level Shor Algorithm Simulation 132 Qubits • 1.70M Gates
Factored generic 31-bit semiprimes (N = 1,537,596,787) by exact simulation of every gate in semiclassical Ekerå–Håstad circuits in 77 s on an Apple M1 Pro using bit-sliced branch evaluation.
High-Throughput Detector Sampling 9–12× Faster Than Stim
FastSampler Clifford detector sampling runs 9–12× faster than natively compiled AVX2 Stim 1.16 on x86 for ≥ 105 shots, verified for bit-for-bit distribution fidelity.
Weight-6 Quantum LDPC Code Discovery 1,000 Groups Searched
Exhaustive group-algebra search discovering [[224,18,12]] and [[288,34,8]] weight-6 qLDPC codes beyond previously published records, and exactly proving the distance of [[288,16,16]].
Automated Cost-Model Engine Planner 11 Simulation Backends
Dynamically routes circuits between 11 specialized engines (AVX2/NEON statevector, Apple Metal GPU, stabilizer tableau, MPS, HSF, sparse, and bit-sliced branches) with minimal held-out regret (1.07–1.11).
Fault-Tolerant Color-Code Syndrome Schedules Distance-8 Certified
Certified d = 9 schedule with circuit distance 8 (improving on Kishony–Fowler's 7), with flagged boundary circuits achieving full distance d at d ∈ {5, 7, 9}.
